Privacy policy

Heimdall is a personal, self-hosted tool that connects the owner's own accounts (Google, Slack, Notion and others) to his personal assistant. Mete Reis Kizildag built it and is its only user. It runs on his own computer and is not offered to anyone else.

Data

  • Heimdall uses Google APIs, including Gmail and Calendar, with the owner's consent and only for the owner's own accounts.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Message text is kept only until it is delivered to the owner's assistant, and event records are deleted after 30 days.
  • No data is sent to anyone other than the owner's own assistant. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
